# Purdue CS Instagram source search — 2026-10-25

Scope: Purdue West Lafayette computing organizations, with Purdue Indianapolis listed separately. The [Purdue CS department roster](https://www.cs.purdue.edu/student-experience/clubs-organizations.html) lists 27 West Lafayette organizations. This search confirms Instagram accounts for 19 of those 27 organizations, plus three ACM subgroups, Build Purdue, LeetCode Club, Purdue Stack, Women in Consulting, and the Indianapolis CS Club. An unresolved roster entry is not evidence that the organization lacks Instagram. Public profile posts were scanned with the project's Instaloader adapter at up to eight recent posts per account. The 45 posts from the four original accounts were already in Snowflake; **177 newly captured posts across 23 accounts were also inserted**. Snowflake now has **222 posts from 27 accounts**. The public-post capture files remain in `/tmp/snowflask-cs-discovery*.json` for audit.
